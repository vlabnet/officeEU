OfficeEU – Free Office for EU
About

OfficeEU is an open-source project designed to support the integration of different systems and digital services used by public administrations across the European Union.

The project aims to provide a common integration layer that makes it easier to connect office applications, government platforms, document management systems, authentication services, and other digital solutions operating within EU public administration.

Goals

Support integration with systems used by EU public administrations.

Provide reusable connectors and integration components.

Improve interoperability between different European digital services.

Simplify document and data exchange between systems.

Support open standards and secure communication.

Reduce duplicated integration work across projects and administrations.

Integrations

The project is intended to support integrations with different systems used by public institutions across the EU. Integrations may include:

Document management systems

E-government platforms

Identity and authentication services

Digital signatures

Public registers

Office and productivity applications

Data exchange platforms

Other administrative and public-sector services

The list of integrations will grow as the project develops.
